% ================= 2. The four precepts =================
% Five overlay pages. Overlay 1 shows four numbered but empty rules, which is a
% real beat: the room can count them before it can read them. Overlays 2 to 5
% land one precept each. The frame ENDS on the fourth precept; no closing line.
%
% Every text slot is wrapped in \onslide (never \only), so the slot is reserved
% on every overlay and no line moves when a later precept lands. That is the
% no-shift rule, SKILL.md Pattern F.
%
% ABRIDGEMENT. Precepts 2 and 3 are cut to fit, and each cut is marked \ldots.
% The full pinned French, for the record and for anyone who wants to restore it:
%
%  2. Le second, de diviser chacune des difficult\'es que j'examinerais, en
%     autant de parcelles qu'il se pourrait, et qu'il serait requis pour les
%     mieux r\'esoudre.
%  3. Le troisi\`eme, de conduire par ordre mes pens\'ees, en commen\c{c}ant par
%     les objets les plus simples et les plus ais\'es \`a conna\^{\i}tre, pour
%     monter peu \`a peu, comme par degr\'es, jusques \`a la connaissance des
%     plus compos\'es.
%
% Precepts 1 and 4 are whole and unabridged.
%
% THE FOUR ALERTED CLAUSES ARE LOAD BEARING. They are the four left-hand cells of
% frame 3, in English above and in this same French below. If you edit one here,
% edit it there in the same pass, and in frame 4 as well.
\begin{frame}{The four precepts}
  \vspace{-2mm}
  {\footnotesize
  % The numerals hang into the tabular's left pad in a zero-width \makebox, so
  % each one sits on the FIRST LINE'S BASELINE of its precept by construction.
  % An earlier build put them in their own r column and beamer's \onslide pushed
  % every precept about 5pt below its numeral. Do not "simplify" this back.
  % hyphenpenalty is set here, outside the cells, so the cell paragraphs inherit
  % it: without it "qu'il se pourrait" breaks as "pour-rait" inside an \alert.
  \hyphenpenalty=10000 \exhyphenpenalty=10000
  \renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.0}%
  \begin{tabular}{@{\hspace{1.5em}}p{0.93\textwidth}@{}}
    \makebox[0pt][r]{{\color{muted}1.}\hspace{0.5em}}\onslide<2->{Le premier \'etait de
        \alert{ne recevoir jamais aucune chose pour vraie},
        que je ne la connusse \'evidemment \^etre telle.\newline
        {\scriptsize\color{muted}Never accept anything as true that I did not
         evidently know to be such.}}\\[2pt]
    \makebox[0pt][r]{{\color{muted}2.}\hspace{0.5em}}\onslide<3->{Le second, de diviser chacune des difficult\'es que
        j'examinerais,
        \alert{en autant de parcelles qu'il se pourrait}~\ldots\newline
        {\scriptsize\color{muted}Divide each difficulty into as many parts as
         I could, and as would be required to resolve it better.}}\\[2pt]
    \makebox[0pt][r]{{\color{muted}3.}\hspace{0.5em}}\onslide<4->{Le troisi\`eme, de conduire par ordre mes pens\'ees, en
        commen\c{c}ant par les objets les plus simples~\ldots\ pour
        \alert{monter peu \`a peu, comme par degr\'es},
        jusques \`a la connaissance des plus compos\'es.\newline
        {\scriptsize\color{muted}Begin with the objects that are simplest and
         climb by degrees to the knowledge of the most composite.}}\\[2pt]
    \makebox[0pt][r]{{\color{muted}4.}\hspace{0.5em}}\onslide<5->{Et le dernier, de faire partout des d\'enombrements si
        entiers, et des revues si g\'en\'erales,
        \alert{que je fusse assur\'e de ne rien omettre}.\newline
        {\scriptsize\color{muted}Everywhere make enumerations so complete, and
         reviews so general, that I could be assured of omitting nothing.}}\\
  \end{tabular}}

  \vspace{1mm}
  {\scriptsize\color{muted}\hspace*{0.2cm}Ren\'e Descartes,
   \textit{Discours de la m\'ethode}, seconde partie (1637).}
\end{frame}
